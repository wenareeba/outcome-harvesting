Who this is for

The library assumes you want to use the method, not only read about it. Here is what it expects of you.

Who it is for

People who run, commission or study a harvest

  • Evaluators designing or running a harvest
  • Commissioners and reviewers judging whether a report stands up
  • Students and researchers learning the method
Before you start

No prior Outcome Harvesting

Lesson 1 starts from what a harvest is for, and each lesson defines its terms with page citations. Some evaluation experience helps with the applied questions.

What it asks of you

Work, not just reading

Each lesson has recall questions, one interactive figure, five applied questions and a writing task with a model answer.

Why it is free

Most Outcome Harvesting training is paid, and the free material is scattered. This library gathers it in one place, cites every page, and corrects itself in public when someone finds an error.

Questions

Do I need to know Outcome Harvesting already?

No. Lesson 1 starts from what a harvest is for and where the method stops. Each later lesson opens with recall questions from the one before, so work through them in order.

How were the sources checked?

A source enters the bibliography only once its title, year and pages, or report series, are confirmed in the document itself or at the publisher, repository or Crossref. Quotations are matched word for word against the source text. Where sources disagree on a citation detail, the errata page shows the evidence.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. On GitHub, choose Code, then Download ZIP. Unzip it and open index.html in a browser; the lessons and cards work without a connection, with system fonts in place of the web fonts.

Can I reuse it in my own teaching?

Yes, with credit. Written material is licensed CC BY 4.0, so you may share and adapt it, including commercially. The code is MIT. Passages quoted from Wilson-Grau and Britt (2013) and Wilson-Grau (2019) stay under their own copyright.

How do I cite it?

Cite the all-versions DOI, 10.5281/zenodo.23514918. It always resolves to the latest version, so the citation stays current as corrections are made.

Wenareeba, Morris. (2026). Outcome Harvesting: Course, Bibliography, Templates and Red Team Protocol. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23514918

GitHub's Cite this repository button gives the same reference from CITATION.cff.

I found an error. What do I do?

Open an issue with the Report a citation error form, or comment where you found the link. You get a first reply within seven days. A confirmed error is corrected within fourteen days and logged in the changelog.

Help keep it accurate.

Check a page citation against your copy of the book, suggest a published harvest, or tell us where a lesson is unclear.
