## 4. The outcome statement

Agree what each outcome statement will contain. Base the decision on one real example from the users' own documents, not a generic one (Book p. 39).

Always included (Book p. 38):

- [ ] **Outcome**: who changed what, when and where
- [ ] **Contribution**: how the intervention influenced the change; who did what with whom that plausibly affected it

Usually included:

- [ ] **Significance**: why the outcome matters for what the intervention is trying to achieve (Book p. 38)

Optional. Add one only if it helps answer a prime question, because every extra field adds work for each outcome (Book pp. 40 to 42):

- [ ] Sources, for each part of the statement
- [ ] Collaboration with other actors in the activity
- [ ] Contribution of other actors to the outcome
- [ ] Contribution of other factors (social, economic, political, environmental)
- [ ] History
- [ ] Context
- [ ] Impact on people's lives or the environment
- [ ] Classification by the intervention's own categories
