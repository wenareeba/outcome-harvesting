Book Figure 3.3, p. 56

The protagonist test

Who is the subject of the change? The intervention: not an outcome. Someone it influenced: it may be. Write in the active voice with that actor as subject. Book p. 57

Step 3: ping-pong with sources

  • Sources know, care and have time; often field staff, not managers Book pp. 64 to 65
  • Sources own the statements Book p. 65
  • On record, with consent, from the start Brief p. 14
  • Usually 2 to 4 rounds Book p. 67

First ping, colleague: send the drafted form, one question per gap. Second, expert: catch activities, awareness, carrying on. Last, decision maker: agree the wording. Book pp. 71 to 78

Done when SMART

  • Specific: who changed what, when, where
  • Measurable: how much, how many
  • Achieved: a plausible link to the change agent
  • Relevant: a significant step towards the impact sought
  • Timely: within the period covered

Brief pp. 10 to 11

Accept only statements with plausible evidence that could be substantiated. Brief p. 14
