Related approaches card

The essentials from Lesson 8. "Book" is Wilson-Grau (2019).

Roots

  • Concepts from Outcome Mapping; process from Utilization-Focused Evaluation; professional evaluation standards Book pp. 18, 26
  • A goal-free method Book p. 23

Outcome Mapping, the parent

Mainly planning; tracks agreed progress markers in boundary partners. OH keeps the sphere of influence and outcomes as behaviour change. Book pp. 18, 193

Most Significant Change, the sibling

Stories selected by panels; surfaces values; no predefined indicators. Davies and Dart 2005, pp. 8, 12 OH puts more emphasis on verification and contribution. Britt 2013, p. 12 MSC is weaker for finished programs and accountability reports. Davies and Dart 2005, p. 13

QuIP, the cousin

Same family: both work back from outcomes and avoid precise attribution. QuIP is narrower, focused on beneficiaries, with bias controls such as blindfolding; OH is broader, including monitoring. Copestake 2017

Contribution analysis, the other end

Starts from a theory of change and tests a contribution claim. Mayne 2019, p. 173 Combine: a harvest finds what changed, including the unexpected; contribution analysis tests the outcomes that matter most. BetterEvaluation 2024

Classifications of these methods differ between sources; check what each starts from. Mayne 2019, p. 172; Book p. 23; INTRAC 2017, p. 4
