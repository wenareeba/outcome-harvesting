# Errata in published Outcome Harvesting citations

Citation details that differ between sources. Each row gives the version this repo uses and why. Checked October 2026.

| Source | What circulates | What this repo uses | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wilson-Grau (2019), *Outcome harvesting: Principles, steps, and evaluation applications* | 2018 (publisher and bookseller listings, Crossref, outcomeharvesting.net) | 2019 | The copyright page reads 2019. The 2018 date is the release date, 8 November 2018, given by Crossref |
| Wilson-Grau (2019), same book | Ricardo Wilson-Grau as editor of an edited volume (Crossref record) | Ricardo Wilson-Grau as author | Title page and chapter openers |
| Wilson-Grau (2019), Appendix A | "History of Outcome Harvesting 2001 to 2017" (Emerald bookstore contents) | "History of Outcome Harvesting 2002 to 2017" | Book contents and p. 189 |
| Wilson-Grau and Britt, *Outcome harvesting* (Ford Foundation brief) | Cited as 2012 with the page numbers of the 27-page version (for example, BetterEvaluation's "2012 guide" links to the 27-page file) | 2013, the November 2013 revision, 27 numbered pages | Date line on the cover: May 2012, revised November 2013. A shorter May 2012 version also circulates (USAID Learning Lab copy), so page numbers differ between the two |
| Principle names in Wilson-Grau (2019) | "Facilitate usefulness throughout the harvest"; "Formulate an outcome as an observable or otherwise verifiable change"; "Ensure credible-enough outcomes" (BetterEvaluation, 2024) | "Facilitate usefulness throughout the evaluation" (p. 150); "Formulate an outcome as an observable change" (p. 174); "Rigorously aim for credible-enough outcomes" (p. 183) | The book's chapter headings, Chapters 8 and 9 |
