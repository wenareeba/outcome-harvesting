# Errata in published Outcome Harvesting citations

Citation details that differ between sources, and figures in the book checked against its own arithmetic. Each row gives the version this repo uses and why. Checked October 2026.

| Source | What circulates | What this repo uses | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wilson-Grau (2019), *Outcome harvesting: Principles, steps, and evaluation applications* | 2018 (publisher and bookseller listings, Crossref, outcomeharvesting.net) | 2019 | The copyright page reads 2019. The 2018 date is the release date, 8 November 2018, given by Crossref |
| Wilson-Grau (2019), same book | Ricardo Wilson-Grau as editor of an edited volume (Crossref record) | Ricardo Wilson-Grau as author | Title page and chapter openers |
| Wilson-Grau (2019), Appendix A | "History of Outcome Harvesting 2001 to 2017" (Emerald bookstore contents) | "History of Outcome Harvesting 2002 to 2017" | Book contents and p. 189 |
| Wilson-Grau and Britt, *Outcome harvesting* (Ford Foundation brief) | Cited as 2012 with the page numbers of the 27-page version (for example, BetterEvaluation's "2012 guide" links to the 27-page file) | 2013, the November 2013 revision, 27 numbered pages | Date line on the cover: May 2012, revised November 2013. A shorter May 2012 version also circulates (USAID Learning Lab copy), so page numbers differ between the two |
| Principle names and numbers in Wilson-Grau (2019) | Two versions in the book itself. Chapter 1 (pp. 14 to 15) and the rubric at the start of each step chapter: I "Facilitate usefulness throughout the harvest", II coach, III nurture, IX "Ensure credible-enough outcomes". Contents page and Chapters 8 and 9: I "throughout the evaluation" (p. 150), II "Nurture appropriate participation" (p. 155), III "Coach human sources to formulate outcome statements" (p. 158), IX "Rigorously aim for credible-enough outcomes" (p. 183). BetterEvaluation (2024) follows Chapter 1, but gives VII as "an observable or otherwise verifiable change" | Chapter 1's names and numbering (pp. 14 to 15), the first full statement and the one BetterEvaluation and the step chapters use. VII as "Formulate an outcome as an observable change", as in both versions in the book | The book's pages as cited; BetterEvaluation approach page, updated 27 June 2024 |

## Figures checked against the book's own arithmetic

Numbers in the book that do not agree with each other or with a standard calculation. The course teaches the corrected figure and says so.

| Where | What the book says | What the check shows | How the course handles it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wilson-Grau (2019), p. 45 | Document review at five pages an hour in the text | The worked example and Table 2.4 on the same page use seven pages an hour | Lesson 3's estimator offers both rates and explains the difference |
| Wilson-Grau (2019), p. 93 | A random sample of 97 outcomes at 95% confidence and a ±3% margin would need "over half (51)" | The standard formula with a finite population correction gives about 89; a sample of 51 gives a margin of about ±9.5% | Lesson 5 gives the corrected figure, which strengthens the book's case against random sampling |
